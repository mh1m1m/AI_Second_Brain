## Corrections made for the two conditions (branch `sb/review-sb010-sb011`)

**RV-07, SB-010.** Every classification function now accepts actual `DataClass` members only and raises `ClassificationError` for anything else, including integers in and out of range, booleans, strings, floats and members of other enumerations. The clearance flags of `storable` must be real booleans, so a truthy string cannot stand in for a clearance. `highest` rejects empty, non-iterable and mixed input. `from_label` is the single conversion from text and accepts exactly `K0` to `K4`. The K1 connector ceiling and the K4 storage prohibition are unchanged and re-tested.

**RV-08, SB-011.** `expires_at`, `is_expired` and `envelope` all require timezone-aware datetimes and normalise to UTC. A lifetime must be a `timedelta` longer than zero and at most 90 days. A submission time more than five minutes later than the clock is refused, because it would otherwise lengthen a note's life. `servable` is the fail-closed form for retrieval paths: it returns True only for a provably unexpired note and False for every unusable input, without raising. Tests cover offsets from −11:30 to +14:00, the exact expiry instant, and the limits of the calendar.

The 90-day maximum and the five-minute tolerance are provisional engineering choices (decision PD-012), not owner approvals.

Tests: `tests/unit/test_domain_adversarial.py`. The suite went from 253 to 346 tests; coverage of `src` remains 100% of statements and branches, which says nothing about components that do not exist yet.
